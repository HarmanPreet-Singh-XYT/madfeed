## Bugs found while building this phase
- Several of the sweeps' first versions proved little: viewers got "bad request" before the role check ran for 23 of 49 routes (fixed by generating valid sample bodies from the contract); the freshness check of the bounce webhook was not independently tested (fixed, mutation now caught).
- The redaction timing test failed only under the race detector (the detector slows regular expressions about 20 times); its budget now accounts for it.
- I committed twice while a check was failing (a formatting complaint, then a race-detector timing test). Both were fixed in the next commit; commits are now gated on a green check.
